Privacy

Last updated 7 October 2026. This page describes what the site does today. It will be updated before payments are added.

The short version

  • You can browse AdDoctor without an account. If you create one, we keep your email address so you can sign in. Nothing else about you is asked for.
  • It sets no cookies and loads no analytics unless you press Accept on the analytics banner. It has no advertising scripts.
  • Photos, links and text you submit to an AI feature are sent to the AI services listed below so they can produce the result. AdDoctor itself does not store them afterwards.
  • If part of the app breaks, a short crash report (the error and your browser version, nothing you typed or uploaded) is sent to our server so it can be fixed.

Accounts

Accounts are handled by Supabase, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany. If you sign up with email, Supabase stores your email address and a scrambled (hashed) form of your password; we never see the password itself. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address, name and profile picture, and nothing else from your Google account. Your browser keeps a sign-in token in local storage so you stay signed in; signing out removes it.

What stays in your browser

Your favourite templates, the reports you save to the Vault and your free credits are kept in your browser's local storage on this device. They are never sent to a server and are not linked to your account, so signing in on another browser does not bring them along. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Each device gets 15 free credits a day. The count is kept in this browser and refills to 15 at midnight your time; there is nothing to buy.

Browsing templates, Examine Ad, the Static Ads studio and "Add my logo" all run in your browser. A logo or ad you load there does not leave your device.

Our server passes these on, returns the result to your browser and keeps no copy. The services above handle the data under their own terms and may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring. Do not upload photos of people, or anything confidential, that you are not entitled to share with them.

The AdDoctor bookmark

The optional AdDoctor bookmark runs only when you click it on a Meta Ad Library page. It reads the public ads shown on that page and hands them to the AdDoctor tab it opens. It does not read your Facebook account, messages, or anything outside the Ad Library results.

Analytics, only if you accept

The first time you visit, a banner asks whether AdDoctor may use analytics cookies. If you decline, nothing is loaded. If you accept, Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics are loaded. They set cookies and tell us which screens and features are used: for example that a template was opened, an ad was generated, or a competitor analysis finished, and how many ads it covered. They are never sent your photos, the text you type, the links you paste, or the ads you make. Google processes this data under its own terms.

Your answer is remembered in your browser.

Crash reports

When part of AdDoctor breaks in your browser, it sends a short report to our server on Cloudflare so the fault can be found and fixed. A report holds:

  • the error message and where in AdDoctor's own code it happened (file names and line numbers),
  • which screen you were on (for example the Vault), without the rest of the address,
  • which version of AdDoctor was running, and your browser's name and version as your browser itself reports it.

It never includes your account or email address, your photos, the text or links you entered, or the ads you made. At most five reports are sent per visit. The server only writes them to its logs at Cloudflare, which hosts it, and they are kept there for a few days at most. If you accepted analytics, the error message and the part of the app it came from also go to Google Tag Manager, like the other events described above.

Technical data

  • Abuse limits. Our server on Cloudflare uses your IP address to count requests per hour, so one visitor cannot run up unlimited costs. The count expires within a day. Cloudflare also keeps short-lived request logs.
  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages, which logs visitor IP addresses for security, as any web host does.
  • Fonts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when the page loads.

Your choices

You can use the template gallery and the in-browser tools without sending anything to a server. AdDoctor keeps no stored copy of the photos, links or text you submit.

If you have an account, you can delete it yourself: open the account menu (your initial or picture at the top right) and choose Delete account. That removes your account and email address from Supabase. If the option is not there yet, or it does not work, ask for the deletion through the contact link below. GitHub issues are public, so do not post your email address there; say which way you signed in (email or Google) and we will tell you how to confirm it's yours.

If you are in the EU or UK you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.
