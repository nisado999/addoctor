Privacy

Last updated 24 October 2026. This page describes what the site does today.

The short version

  • You can browse AdDoctor without an account. If you create one, we keep your email address so you can sign in. Nothing else about you is asked for.
  • It sets no cookies and loads no analytics unless you press Accept on the analytics banner. It has no advertising scripts.
  • Photos, links and text you submit to an AI feature are sent to the AI services listed below so they can produce the result. AdDoctor itself does not store them afterwards.
  • If part of the app breaks, a short crash report (the error and your browser version, nothing you typed or uploaded) is sent to our server so it can be fixed.

Accounts

Accounts are handled by Supabase, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany. If you sign up with email, Supabase stores your email address and a scrambled (hashed) form of your password; we never see the password itself. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address, name and profile picture, and nothing else from your Google account. Your browser keeps a sign-in token in local storage so you stay signed in; signing out removes it.

What stays in your browser

Your favourite templates, the reports you save to the Vault and your free credits are kept in your browser's local storage on this device. They are not sent to a server and are not linked to your account, so signing in on another browser does not bring them along. Clearing your browser data removes them.

The creatives you save in Competitor Spy are kept the same way: only a short list of Ad Library ad numbers, in this browser, so the page can show which ones you marked. The ads themselves are not stored.

Without an account, each device gets 15 free credits a day. The count is kept in this browser and refills to 15 at midnight your time. When credits are charged to an account (see Payments and credits below), your balance is kept in your account instead.

Browsing templates, Examine Ad, the Static Ads studio, the Idea Engine and placing your logo on a finished ad all run in your browser. A logo or ad you load there, and the product details you type into the Idea Engine, do not leave your device. The Idea Engine remembers what you typed in this browser so your ideas are there next time.

Payments and credits

When you buy a plan or a top-up, the payment is handled by Paddle, which is the seller of record. Paddle collects your card or other payment details, billing address and any tax details, and processes them under its own terms and privacy policy. We never see or store your card number. We receive your email address, which plan you bought, whether the subscription is active, the dates, and Paddle's transaction and subscription identifiers. To match the payment to your account, the checkout also passes your AdDoctor account id (a random number, not your name) to Paddle.

Your account keeps a credit balance and a ledger of changes (credits granted, spent, returned or expired, with the action that used them) so that your balance is correct and any dispute can be checked. This is stored with Supabase, in Frankfurt, with your account. It does not include the content of your photos, links or questions. If you delete your account, the balance and ledger are deleted with it; Paddle keeps its own records of payments for as long as the law requires.

If you answer "which describes you best" on the Plans page (shop owner, freelancer or marketer, agency), that answer is stored with your account and used only to decide what to build.

The AdDoctor bookmark

The optional AdDoctor bookmark runs only when you click it on a Meta Ad Library page. It reads the public ads shown on that page and hands them to the AdDoctor tab it opens. It does not read your Facebook account, messages, or anything outside the Ad Library results.

Analytics, only if you accept

The first time you visit, a banner asks whether AdDoctor may use analytics cookies. If you decline, nothing is loaded. If you accept, Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics are loaded. They set cookies and tell us which screens and features are used: for example that a template was opened, an ad was generated, or a competitor analysis finished, and how many ads it covered. They are never sent your photos, the text you type, the links you paste, or the ads you make. Google processes this data under its own terms.

Your answer is remembered in your browser.

Crash reports

When part of AdDoctor breaks in your browser, it sends a short report to our server on Cloudflare so the fault can be found and fixed. A report holds:

  • the error message and where in AdDoctor's own code it happened (file names and line numbers),
  • which screen you were on (for example the Vault), without the rest of the address,
  • which version of AdDoctor was running, and your browser's name and version as your browser itself reports it.

It never includes your account or email address, your photos, the text or links you entered, or the ads you made. At most five reports are sent per visit. The server only writes them to its logs at Cloudflare, which hosts it, and they are kept there for a few days at most. If you accepted analytics, the error message and the part of the app it came from also go to Google Tag Manager, like the other events described above.

Technical data

  • Abuse limits. Our server on Cloudflare counts requests per hour and per day, by your account when you are signed in and by your IP address when you are not, so one visitor cannot run up unlimited costs. The counts expire within a day. Cloudflare also keeps short-lived request logs.
  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages, which logs visitor IP addresses for security, as any web host does.
  • Fonts. Fonts are served from AdDoctor's own site. Your browser does not contact Google Fonts.

Why we use your data

  • To provide what you ask for (your account, the AI features you run, your plan and credits): this is needed to perform our agreement with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep the service safe and working (abuse limits, crash reports, short-lived logs): our legitimate interest in preventing abuse and fixing faults (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Analytics: only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time with the "Ask me again" button above.
  • Payment records: Paddle keeps these because tax and accounting law requires it (Art. 6(1)(c)).

Data processed outside the EU

Supabase keeps your account in Frankfurt. Some of the other services listed on this page are based in the United States or run worldwide (Anthropic, Google, ScrapeCreators, Cloudflare, GitHub and Paddle), so data you send to a feature can be processed outside the EU. They handle it under their own data processing terms. Ask us (see Contact) if you want to know the safeguards for a specific service.

How long data is kept

  • Your account, email address, plan, credit balance and ledger: until you delete your account.
  • Sign-in check on our server: 5 minutes. Ad scores: up to 30 days. Request counts: up to a day. Crash reports and request logs at Cloudflare: a few days.
  • Payment records at Paddle: as long as the law requires.
  • Everything kept in your browser: until you clear your browser data.

Your choices and rights

You can use the template gallery and the in-browser tools without sending anything to a server. AdDoctor keeps no stored copy of the photos, links or text you submit.

You have the right to see the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, and to object to or limit how we use it. Ask through the contact below and we will answer within a month.

If you have an account, you can delete it yourself: open the account menu (your initial or picture at the top right) and choose Delete account. That removes your account, email address, credit balance and ledger from Supabase. If you are on a paid plan, cancel it first from the link in your Paddle receipt, so you are not charged again. If the option is not there yet, or it does not work, ask for the deletion through the contact link below. GitHub issues are public, so do not post your email address there; say which way you signed in (email or Google) and we will tell you how to confirm it's yours.

If you are in the EU or UK you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.
