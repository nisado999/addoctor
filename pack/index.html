A month of posts from one product

Social Pack turns a product description, your photos or a store link into a month of organic posts: the picture, the words on it, and the caption.

Open Social Pack

How it works

  1. Tell it about the product. Write a short description, add product photos, or paste your store link.
  2. Get a brief. AdDoctor writes a creative brief with one consistent photographic style, so the month looks like one brand.
  3. Generate the posts. A mix of formats: hero shots, lifestyle, quick tips, details, benefit callouts and more, each with a caption and a strong first line.
  4. Check and fix each one. Every post can be scored, edited by instruction and regenerated before you download it.

Rules it follows

It never invents discounts, prices, statistics, awards or reviews, and it keeps the words on each image short and spelled exactly.

Questions

Is the product kept exactly as it is?

When you add photos, the posts use your real product as the source of truth for its shape, colour and logo.

Which language is used?

The language of your product description, unless you choose another.
