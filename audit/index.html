Examine Ad checks a creative the way a media buyer would, then hands you a prescription: what is working, what is leaking, and what to change first.

Examine an ad

How it works

  1. Bring your ad. Add the headline and copy, and your image if you have one. Examine Ad runs in your browser.
  2. Get the score. You receive an overall score out of 100 and a plain status for how ready the ad is.
  3. Read the prescription. A short list of fixes, most important first, that you can copy or save to your Vault.
  4. Rebuild it. Start from a template that matches the ad's angle and make a stronger version.

What it looks at

  • The hook: does the first line earn the next second?
  • Clarity: is it obvious what is being sold and to whom?
  • Proof and offer: is there a reason to believe it and a reason to act now?
  • The creative itself: contrast, legibility on a phone, clutter.

Good to know

A score is a starting point for a conversation with your data, not a replacement for testing. Use it to decide what to test first.

Questions
