Refund policy

Last updated 16 October 2026.

The short version

  • Ask within 14 days of a payment and you can have it back if you have used fewer than 10% of the credits it gave you.
  • If we charge you by mistake, or something we did stops you using what you paid for, we refund it in full.
  • If an action fails, you do not lose the credits: they are returned automatically.

Subscriptions

The first payment for a plan (monthly or yearly) can be refunded within 14 days if you have used fewer than 10% of that plan's credits. After that, or once you have used more, payments are not refundable, because the credits have been used and they cost us money to provide. You can cancel at any time so you are not charged again; the plan keeps working until the period you paid for ends.

Renewals

If a renewal charged you and you did not mean to continue, tell us within 7 days of the charge and you have not used the new credits, and we will refund it.

Top-ups

A top-up can be refunded within 14 days if none of its credits have been used.

Your legal rights

These rules are in addition to the rights you have by law, which we do not limit. If you are in the EU or UK and buy for personal use, you can have a right to cancel within 14 days. By using credits straight away you ask us to start providing the service immediately, and you accept that this ends the right to cancel for the credits you have used.

How to ask

Use the link in your payment receipt from Paddle, or open an issue on the AdDoctor GitHub page (do not post your email or order details there; say which plan and date, and we will tell you how to confirm it is you). Refunds are paid back to the original payment method by Paddle, usually within 5 to 10 business days.
