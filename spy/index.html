See what a competitor is really running

Type a brand's name or paste its Meta Ad Library link. AdDoctor reads the ads that advertiser is running, lays the creatives out as a gallery, and tells you what each one is doing.

Open Competitor Spy

How it works

  1. Find the brand. Search by name, or paste a link from the Meta Ad Library. Pick the right page from the results.
  2. Read the ads. AdDoctor pulls the brand's ads (up to 1,000 in one run), groups near-identical ones, and looks at the most-used and longest-running creatives.
  3. Open a creative. Each one gets a breakdown: the hook, the problem it points at, the solution, the proof, the offer and the call to action, plus the awareness stage and who it is aimed at.
  4. Ask questions. "Which hooks repeat most?" or "What are they discounting this month?" The answer comes only from the ads that were read, and shows the ads it used.
  5. See the organic side. Add the brand's Instagram or TikTok handle to see its best posts ranked by likes, comments and shares.

What you get

  • A summary of the brand's main focus, top products, promotions and sales.
  • A filterable gallery: static, video or carousel, running 30 days or more, newest, most versions.
  • A per-creative breakdown you can copy into your own brief.
  • Save the creatives you want to come back to.

Good to know

An ad library shows what is running, not what it spends or earns, so long-running ads are a signal, not proof, of what works. For video ads AdDoctor reads the cover frame, not the motion or the sound.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. You can try Competitor Spy without signing in.

Where do the ads come from?

From Meta's public Ad Library. Nothing is read from anyone's private account.

Which platforms does it cover?

Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads for the ads side, and Instagram and TikTok for the organic posts.
